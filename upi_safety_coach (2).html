UPI SAFETY — 5 GOLDEN RULES

STOP • CHECK • NEVER RUSH
🔒 1. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.
📥 2. You do not enter a PIN to receive money.
🔍 3. Verify names and payment requests before approving.
🚫 4. Avoid unknown links and screen-sharing apps.
☎️ 5. Contact your bank using official channels.
